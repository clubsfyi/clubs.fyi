What is clubs.fyi?

clubs.fyi is a list of communities you can join in your city.

New or old, big or small, a club is any group that fosters human connection.

No accounts, no ads, no noise, only clubs you can join today.

Who is behind clubs.fyi?

clubs.fyi was created by Alex Morales, but the project belongs to everybody.

Can I help?

Yes. Send reviews of clubs you tried or suggest ones we miss. Help us curate the list, your part makes the whole better.

What does it mean for a club to be vouched?

A vouched club is one we tried ourselves. A person from our team joined for real, became an active member, became a regular.

They felt connection comes first, people gathered around a common interest. Not a performative act, nothing to show off, just the joy of doing it together.
